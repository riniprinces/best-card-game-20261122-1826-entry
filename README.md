Ember Duel is an original turn-based card battler: you are Ember, the flame, duelling Frost across a table. Each turn you gain 3 energy and draw a card, play cards from your hand, and Frost answers — it tells you what it will do first, so you can block it and strike back. First to 0 life loses.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 to play (click a card or press 1–7 to play it, Space to end your turn, R to restart).
